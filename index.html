⚠️ CRITICAL LEVEL STRATEGY WARNING

On Floor 4 Map 2: You HAVE TO LOOK AT THE MAP CAREFULLY. If you don't and you just go pushing walls, the secret walls jam the OTHER secret walls and you are completely screwed...

In the red brick room - YOU HAVE TO DO IT IN THIS ORDER BELOW:

Middle ➔ Upper Left ➔ Middle ➔ Lower Right ➔ Upper Left ➔ Push first wall into slot ➔ Lower Right

This will get you to the door where the treasure, lives, and gun is at. Once you do this - go BACK and push some of those remaining walls around (but once you do it will BLOCK THE DOOR so make sure you get everything in that secret area FIRST).

Okay now for the bottom of the red brick room: If you do it wrong you won't get completion. To do this room you need to go all the way back to the beginning and enter the red bricks from that area. Push the first secret wall AND STOP - DO NOT PUSH ANYTHING ELSE.

Now go all the way back to the upper red brick room where you got to the treasure room and push the last secret wall.
